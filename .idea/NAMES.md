# App Name Brainstorm

## Favorites (Round 1)
- **Voto** — Italian "vote/wish." Short, Italy nod, rating mechanic. Strong brand ceiling.
- **Tally** — The scoring angle. Universal, any language, easy to say.
- **Avanti** — Italian "let's go/forward." Energetic, travel-native.
- **Via** — Latin/Italian "road/way." Ultra minimal. Strong brand potential.
- **Overlap** — Literal product value prop. Descriptive, product-y, not generic travel.

## Round 2
- **Hive** — Group intelligence. Everyone contributes, collective output. Works beyond travel.
- **Rally** — Rally the group around a plan. Action-oriented, energetic.
- **Knot** — Tie plans together. Nautical/adventure. Bonus: wedding connection (origin trip).
- **Chorus** — Everyone's voice in harmony. The compatibility matrix = a chorus of opinions.
- **Flock** — Group travel instinct. Where does the flock land?
- **Nexus** — The center point where everyone's plans meet.
- **Pulse** — Feeling the group's interests. "What's the pulse on the Vatican?"
- **Stride** — Moving forward together. Clean, action-forward.
- **Cast** — Cast of travelers + casting a wide net of activities.
- **Braid** — Weaving everyone's plans into one itinerary. Nice visual metaphor.
