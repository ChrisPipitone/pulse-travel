3. Confidence Model

What increases confidence?

- Density of Data: Seeing the "Avatar Row" fill up on an activity card.
- Alignment Confirmation: Seeing a "Universal Favorite" label (zero-friction decision).
- Logistical Certainty: Seeing a "Peak Overlap" window that includes the core group.
- Sub-group Discovery: Finding a "Travel Twin" (ensures at least one other person shares your niche interests).

What decreases confidence?

- Information Asymmetry: "No Signal" labels (uncertainty about group interest).
- Silent Friction: "Split Crowd" labels with no follow-up conversation.
- Missing Agents: Members who have joined but have 0% rating progress.
- Logistical Gaps: Large windows of time where only 1-2 members are present.

---

4. Key Product Moments

- Discovering a "Universal Favorite": The relief of a zero-friction decision. It validates that the group is, at some level, a single unit.
- Finding a "Travel Twin": Reduces the fear of isolation. "Even if the whole group doesn't like this, Sam does, so we can do it together."
- Seeing "Peak Overlap": The transition from "theoretical trip" to "physical reality." It anchors the plan in time.
- Resolving a "Split Crowd": The efficiency of identifying a split early. It allows the group to plan two concurrent tracks without anyone
  feeling "dragged along."

---

5. Product Narrative: The Arc of Confidence

I: The Fog of Choice
"I have no idea what my group wants."
The Lead feels the weight of 200 WhatsApp messages. Information is scattered, emotions are hidden, and the fear of planning a "bad" trip is high.
Confidence is near zero.

II: The Pulse Emerges
"The group is starting to speak."
As ratings flow in, the noise settles into patterns. The Lead stops looking at individual names and starts looking at the Social Signal. They see
that Italy isn't 14 individuals; it's a group that collectively loves "Vatican Museums" but is "Split" on "Hiking."

III: The Final Shape
"I am confident this plan represents the group."
The plan is no longer a guess. It is a mathematical reflection of shared enthusiasm. The Member feels their "MUSTs" are protected, and the Lead
feels the authority to build an itinerary because the social work—the hard part—is already done. Confidence is total.
